## Problem statement

OTT platforms often neglect regional cultures or remain unaffordable for people in India’s Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. This leaves a gap in infotainment and mythological content in regional languages for adults.

## My role

Product Manager — end-to-end ownership

- Led strategy, research, product design, and prototyping.
- Conducted market research, user surveys, persona creation, and PRD development.
- Created the product wireframes and low-fidelity prototype in Figma.

## Discovery and research

- Secondary research suggested a market projected to reach $12.5B by 2030, with strong demand for regional content.
  - **Regional languages:** 70–75% of content consumed is in Hindi and regional languages.
  - **Top segment:** SVoD leads, projected at ₹163.8B in 2024.
  - **User growth:** 634M users by 2029; penetration expected to rise from 34.1% to 42.2%.
  - **Monetisation:** ARPU expected at ₹670.20 in 2024.

![Market research on India’s OTT audience](/content/work/lok-learn/market-research-1.png)

![OTT market and regional content research](/content/work/lok-learn/market-research-2.png)

- Surveyed 10+ users from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities, analysing device usage, preferred content, and pain points.
- Defined four key personas, including a homemaker from Asansol and a college student from Sambalpur.

## Product vision

> To be the leading OTT platform in regional languages for Bharat, offering infotainment and mythological content that promotes holistic learning at an affordable price.

![Lok Learn product identity](/content/work/lok-learn/product-identity.png)

## Prioritised solutions

![Feature prioritisation for Lok Learn](/content/work/lok-learn/prioritised-solutions.png)

- Infotainment and mythology playlists.
- Certificates and quizzes after a playlist.
- Regional-language UI and subtitles.
- Offline downloads and tiered subscription pricing.
- Auto-play, suggestions, trailers, and a watchlist.

What sets the app apart is skill development and mythological content in regional languages, available through a tiered subscription model.

## Hypothesis and success metrics

**Hypothesis:** Providing downloadable certificates improves customer engagement and retention by 10% in one month.

Measures tied to the hypothesis:

1. Number of certificates downloaded.
2. Accuracy of quiz answers.
3. Video replay and pause frequency.
4. Number of videos watched.
5. Number of quizzes taken.
6. Certificates uploaded to LinkedIn with Lok Learn tagged.
7. Time spent in the app.
8. User feedback and CSAT.
9. Churn rate.
10. Drop-off while watching a video.
11. Drop-off while taking a quiz.
12. Acceptance rate for the quiz shown at the end of a playlist.
