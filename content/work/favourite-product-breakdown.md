The prompt was to take a favourite app, break down its design, critique what works and what does not, and suggest improvements. My choice was **Pocket**.

![Pocket’s article discovery interface](/content/work/favourite-product-breakdown/pocket-interface.png)

## What the app does well

### It is useful

Pocket makes it easy to read articles and blogs without subscribing to multiple newsletters.

### It is inventive

- It offers a wide range of categories and a personalised dashboard based on reading habits.
- Its text-to-speech feature lets someone listen to an article like a podcast.

### It is easy to understand

The interface is clean, and the main calls to action are clear.

## What could be better

1. Show definitions or synonyms for complicated words.
2. Add a sleep timer.
3. Let users queue articles for continuous narration.
