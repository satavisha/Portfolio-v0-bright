## Background

KPHealth is an app concept designed to help Kaiser Permanente members stay on top of their health routines, with a special focus on diabetes prevention and treatment.

## Initial focus

- High risk of preventable disease:
  - 34M people, or 10.5% of the US population, have diabetes.
  - 90–95% have Type 2 diabetes.
  - 88M have prediabetes, and most are uninformed.
- The US is becoming increasingly health-aware, especially among younger generations.

![KPHealth market context and initial focus](/content/work/kphealth/initial-focus.png)

- High cost of healthcare:
  - $327B in expenditure on diabetes treatment.
  - 66% of the US population fears that they cannot afford healthcare.

## Opportunity

- **$430B market:** A significant opportunity in the US diabetes market.
- **$7B sector:** The US health-coaching market presents strong growth potential.
- **Digital advantage:** Digital tools create an opportunity to stand out in a rapidly digitising healthcare landscape.

![Average US healthcare expenditure per capita](/content/work/kphealth/expenditure.png)

## Roadmap pillars

The app focuses on preventative care: forming habits, improving quality of life, lowering healthcare costs, and connecting users with experts for tailored support.

Strategic pillars:

- Personalised healthcare plans.
- Community building.
- Greater health awareness.

## Expanding programmes

- Onboard more preventable diseases, including heart disease and PCOS.
- Improve the AI chatbot.
- Connect a 24/7 hotline.
- Partner with gyms, local supermarkets, and e-commerce companies.

## Widening the scope

- Enhance core functionality.
- Use AI for chatbots and targeted health advice.
- Integrate smartwatches and pulse oximeters for more accurate results.
- Build a marketplace connecting doctors, dieticians, and coaches.
- Extend the product into mental wellbeing.

## Marketing and pricing strategy

### Acquisition channels

- **App stores:** Make the product accessible through both the Apple App Store and Google Play.
- **Influencer marketing:** Partner with health and wellness creators who have authentic audience relationships.
- **SEO:** Improve content and site structure to reach the target market.
- **Email marketing:** Reach KP members directly.
- **Healthcare touchpoints:** Place advertising in hospitals, diagnostic centres, and fitness facilities.

### Revenue goal

The product aims to raise health awareness, encourage healthier lifestyles, and reduce the cost of diabetes treatment through preventative care. The pricing concept uses a $5-per-month freemium model; partnerships with gyms could provide an additional revenue path.

## Preparing for launch

### Pre-launch checklist

1. Legal — review privacy issues.
2. Marketing — prepare the blog post, release notes, and screenshots.
3. Engineering — complete testing and launch readiness.
4. Leadership — communicate the launch plan.
5. Metrics — define downloads, stability, usage, and retention.
6. App stores — submit the product for review.

### Risks

- **Security breach:** Keep data security in place and give it high priority.
- **No post-launch plan:** Observe behaviour and maintain a clear improvement plan after release.
- **Technical issues:** Test repeatedly and include a post-launch response plan.
