## Parent and child pain points

1. Parents are concerned that children spend too much time on screens.
2. Multitasking from an early age can diminish a child’s concentration.
3. A digital generation gap separates parents and children.
4. High screen time increases the risk of childhood obesity.
5. Screen addiction can reduce real-life social interaction.
6. Social media can create pressure around appearance and behaviour.
7. Instant gratification from technology can make self-discipline harder to build.

![Pew Research Center chart about parenting in the age of screens](/content/work/parentry/research.png)

## Potential responses

- Track phone usage.
- Restrict harmful language and disturbing media.
- Encourage more family time.
- Encourage physical activity.

## The concept

**GetFit by Parentry** is a feature concept for a parenting app that helps track children’s health.

### Initial focus

- Children may eat more while using screens.
- Advertising for high-calorie, low-nutrition foods can affect preferences and eating habits.
- Screen exposure can disrupt sleep and, in turn, appetite and mental wellbeing.
- Children drawn to online games may choose them over outdoor play and socialising.

### Proposal

- Encourage healthy lifestyle choices.
- Promote physical activity for children.
- Offer meditation for parents and children.
- Suggest healthy, fun recipes.
- Encourage shared outdoor activities, such as offline games and evening walks.

## Total addressable market

The source estimate used the following inputs:

- Around 400M people in India aged 25–50.
- 90% of people aged 25–50 are married.
- 5% of couples are childless.
- 35% of India’s population is urban.

The resulting estimate was approximately 120M people.

## Design sprint

### Background

Parentry helps parents make informed choices about their child’s digital activity and balance work with schooling.

### Problem

Children spend too much time online, affecting sleep cycles, eating habits, and physical wellbeing.

### Solution

Introduce a feature that encourages regular physical activity, helps parents manage a child’s diet using BMI, and provides meditation support.

## Success metrics

- Parents’ active app-usage time.
- Number of activities logged by a parent.
- Number of weeks in which a child’s weight is updated.
- Product feedback.

## Feature scope

GetFit recommends activities based on a child’s age, weight, and height. P0 indicates high priority, P1 medium priority, and P2 low priority.

| Priority | Feature | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Welcome | A welcome page for parents to sign in |
| P0 | Log physical activity | A page where parents can log daily activities |
| P0 | Evaluate a child’s health | Calculate BMI by age and recommend lifestyle changes |
| P0 | Progress | Display cumulative health-progress data |
| P1 | Meal recommendations | Recommend meals to parents |
| P1 | Activity recommendations | Suggest social activities for parents and children |
| P1 | Meditations | Recommend guided meditations for parents and children |
| P2 | Articles | Recommend health-related reading |
| P2 | Community | Let parents share stories, comment, and connect |
